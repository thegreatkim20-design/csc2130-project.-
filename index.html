Clinic Appointment System

The Clinic Appointment System is a web-based platform designed to help patients easily book, manage, and track their clinic appointments online. It aims to reduce long queues and improve the efficiency of healthcare service delivery.

Features

  • Book clinic appointments online from anywhere
  • View and manage upcoming appointment schedules
  • Receive automatic appointment reminders
  • Access personal medical visit history
  • Contact clinic staff directly through the platform
  • Cancel or reschedule appointments with ease
